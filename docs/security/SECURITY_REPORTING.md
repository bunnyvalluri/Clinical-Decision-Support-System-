# Security Reporting & Disclosure Policy

## Reporting Standard
1. **Internal Disclosure Only**: Security reports generated by `Agentic-Bug-Hunter` or `SecurityOrchestrator` are strictly internal to the hospital's engineering and clinical leadership.
2. **No Autonomous External Submissions**: Vulnerabilities are never automatically submitted to HackerOne, Bugcrowd, public issue trackers, or external bug bounty platforms.
3. **Dual-Custody Sign-Off**: Reports must be reviewed and signed by an authorized IT Administrator before being distributed to engineering or executive stakeholders.
4. **Report Structure**:
   - Title & Severity (validated)
   - Affected Asset & Environment
   - Vulnerability Description & Impact
   - Sanitized Evidence & Reproduction PoC
   - Root Cause Analysis & Remediation Steps
   - Retest Status
