# Explainable AI (XAI) & TreeSHAP — BPY-CSE-2666

## Core Distinction: Attribution vs. Causation
Feature attributions generated by TreeSHAP or KernelSHAP represent statistical associations learned by the model from clinical cohorts. **They do not prove biological etiology or medical causation.**

## Technical Architecture
1. **TreeSHAP Integration:** Operates directly on the ensembled decision trees of `RandomForestClassifier`.
2. **Individualized Attributions:** For each patient prediction, SHAP computes positive (risk-elevating) and negative (protective) factor contributions.
3. **Clinical Explanation Panel:** Renders accessible visual waterfall bars accompanied by plain-English descriptions for attending clinicians.
4. **Mandatory Disclaimer:** Every explanation payload includes the legal disclaimers safeguarding against uncritical automated decision reliance.
